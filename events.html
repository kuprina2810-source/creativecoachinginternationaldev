Events & Retreats

Coaching doesn't only happen one-to-one. Come together for walks, trips, and gatherings designed to help you reconnect — with yourself and with other women on the journey.

The offering

Space to slow down and reset.

Intimate getaways and workshops built around real rest, real connection, and tools that travel home with you. No agenda — just space to breathe, reflect, and realign.

From sailing days and wellness retreats to community gatherings and organizational programs, every event is designed to deepen the journey.
